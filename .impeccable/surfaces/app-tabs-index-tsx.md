# Surface brief: Noor+ app shell (all tabs)

Mode: Operate. Platform: adaptive (iOS HIG / Material 3 behaviours, one lacquer world). Audience: Maldivian Muslims glancing at prayer times many times a day, en/dv/ar, RTL.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A prayer app is a lathe-turned lacquer vessel (liyelaa jehun), not a night sky: one saturated vermilion object carries the next prayer; incised triple bands, not waves and sparkles, are the only ornament. Refuses the navy-and-glow "Islamic app" default.
OWN-WORLD: Lacquer-black ground (warm, never blue) with bone text, vermilion as the committed colour, ochre-yellow incised lines, limewash coral-stone grey for Day. Cards are plain matte vessels with hairline borders; the signature is a three-line band (thin / thick / thin) used as divider, hero edge, tab-bar rim and active-row mark. Bricolage Grotesque numerals, system text, Naskh for Arabic.
STORY: In one second you know which prayer is next and how long remains; the rest of the day is a quiet ledger where passed prayers recede.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Island + dual date on the left, nothing else. Full-width vermilion hero: prayer name, time, huge countdown, an ochre progress band showing how far the current gap has run. Below, the six-row ledger with larger tabular times; the active row carries the band mark, passed rows dim.
FORM: Candidate 3 of 7 (liyelaa lacquerwork), seed: seed:7d9374811189e537. Others considered: coral-stone carving, thundu kunaa mat weave, lagoon aerial, dhoni, ferry board, Thaana typewriter.
EXTENSION: Qibla compass becomes a turned dish of concentric bands; calendar today-card and settings/duas headers reuse the band header; tab bar is platform-adaptive (iOS: band rim + tinted icon; Android: M3 pill indicator).
RISK: Red-on-black can read aggressive; vermilion is limited to hero, selected states and the band mark, with ochre carrying quiet accents.
